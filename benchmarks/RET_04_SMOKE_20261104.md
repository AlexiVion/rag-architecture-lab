# RET-04 — Exploratory Parent-Child retrieval smoke (2026-11-04)

**Status: exploratory, not a canonical architecture comparison.** Results are transcribed from a completed local Windows PowerShell verification run of commit `9cff4f7`. The local full JSON has not been published or independently re-run.

- Dataset: `nano-beir/scifact`, 2,919 indexed documents in the log.
- Queries: the first 10 eligible benchmark queries.
- Baseline: in-memory BM25.
- Candidate: deterministic Parent-Child chunk adapter with BM25, `chunk_chars=512`, `overlap_chars=64`, `candidate_k=100`.
- Artifact saved locally: `benchmarks/results/RET-04-smoke_20261104T124640Z.json`.
- Quality comparison uses identical corpus and qrels on that run; warm-up policy and statistical uncertainty are not established.

## Interpretation

On this **10-query smoke** Parent-Child decreased ranking/recall metrics and increased latency. This is a negative observation within the tested configuration, *not* proof that Parent-Child generally underperforms BM25.

## Next experiment

Expand to the complete designated benchmark query set, vary chunk length/overlap and candidate depth, retain per-query error cases, and compare against matched baseline and uncertainty estimates. Only then decide GO/NO-GO.
